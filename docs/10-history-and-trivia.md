Dates are from the RS Wiki's update-history sections for the GOP, the Guild, the token, the
barrier generators and the omni-talisman.

| 2 Sep 2008 | Worlds 60 (members) and 61 (free) suggested as GOP worlds |
| 25 Feb 2009 | Barriers can no longer block an altar's exit portal |
| 8 Jul 2009 | Free players no longer penalised for members-only altars |
| 2 Sep 2009 | Barriers placed while moving stay where they were placed |

| 7 Sep 2010 | Female robes adjusted to match male versions |
| 6 Oct 2010 | Teleport-tablet purchase inventory check adjusted |

2010) says it "really impacted hard on people".

## The community

* **Vief's Trapdoor** ([yongyi781/great-orb-project](https://github.com/yongyi781/great-orb-project)):
  a fan-made GOP emulator website with solo play, multiplayer, puzzles, "dead practice", hiscores, an
  angle-map tool and a chat. It was hosted at greatorbproject.tk / vief.tk, and its repository was
  last updated in December 2023. Its engine is the basis of [05 Part 2](05-tick-engine.md).
* **GOP Returns:** a friends chat and Discord that ran monthly events as of 2022 (AnroyRS video).
* Angle-guide creators on YouTube: ThePuu4, Heather M, Wrestling Science, Chris Rainey, Cerddi,
  Tawin11 ([videos.md](videos.md)).

## Trivia

* The altar exit portals are a nod to Valve's *Portal*. Their examine text is "Look at you,
  examining things when there's Runecrafting to do!" (RS Wiki trivia).
* Several emotes are blocked in the game with orb-themed messages. "Give Thanks" and "Seal of
  Approval" say "prevent", and "Skillcape" says "prevents" (RS Wiki).
* Wizard Vief says "HUGE FAILURE" when his team loses every altar.
* Vief backs up his theory with "Lefebvre equations", a joke on the Legendre transform.
* A barrier over the Fire altar's flames puts the fire out.
* The emulator's code contains the comment "There will be no zero-deading for you!" where it stops
  a player's hold on an orb that has just scored, so you cannot "dead" an orb on the tick it scores.
* Wizard Acantha appeared at the Christmas 2011 banquet, seated next to Bogrog.
