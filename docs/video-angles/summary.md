## Most useful findings
1. **Movement rule.** Orbs move diagonally first, then straight, one tile per orb-tick, and are blocked by arch legs and rocks. An "angle" is an orb position whose diagonal leg lands in a gap: an arch gap at (0,±4)/(±4,0), or the slot between an arch leg and a corner rock (about (±2,±4)/(±4,±2)). This fits every Air clip once orientation is fixed. An orb scores when it reaches the zone edge, so attracting from the far home square works.
2. **The KB pillar model is confirmed** for Air (grid diagrams and simulator), Fire, Body, Water, Earth (at the altar) and Nature. Every confirmed altar has arch legs at (±1,±4)/(±4,±1), a lintel over the gap tile, and corner rocks at (±3,±3).
3. **Mind does not fit the model.** It has a round altar, a "table" dolmen directly S (the guides' (0,-2) is on its north edge), an archway N, and an irregular ring of standing stones 3–6 tiles out. Walled pens hold orbs 8–17 tiles out. Its scoring zone may be smaller than 5×5 (unconfirmed). Mind needs its own map.
4. **Orientation trap.** The four arches look alike, so a stone at the top of the screen doesn't mean north. Many guides film facing W, E or S. Air has one fixed landmark: the white portal/spawn disc at about **(-3,-6)**. I re-oriented the first video by it, and its rows changed from "all S home" to a mix of all four home squares.
5. **Air setups by home square** (Everheather), all mirror N/S. Details are in air.md.
   - **From E home:** "Hold from center" works for orbs at (-1,±6) and (0,±5).
   - **From W home:** holding fails for (1,±5)/(1,±6); tap with **OAO = Orb Altar Orb** instead.
   - **Farther orbs:** step 1–2 tiles away from the orb's side and use run off.
   - **Corner angle:** stand at (±3,±2) next to a corner rock.
6. **Technique vocabulary** is defined in general.md: tap, hold-for-N, step, set, OAO, fake-repel, repel trick, prototick, premove, dead, orb tick, multi-orb, run on/off.
7. **Nature arena** (no prior map data):
   - same inner ring as the model;
   - a **floating exit portal just N of the N stone at about (-1..0, 5..6)**;
   - a dirt path ring at radius about 7–9, then boulders (NW/W) and rings of trees from about 10–12 tiles out;
   - one small pond far NNE at about (6..10, 17..20);
   - orbs appear at random, 4–9 tiles out, and drift into the trees.
8. **Arena hazards per altar:**
   - Fire: lava W/E/NE, tree spawns W/NW.
   - Body: flesh-cave walls N/W/E, pink portal S/SW.
   - Water: water all round, spawns on the NE/E/SE grass and a portal W/NW.
   - Earth: plateaus, stalagmites and chasms outside the ring.
   - Probable Cosmic: maze walls.

## Known uncertainties
- **Compass reading:** in eiipycLhN5M (Mind), PPXA-Xg9qFM's rotated clips, and the Nature video, north comes from a tiny compass. A misread flips those tiles 90°/180°. The rows say which.
- **Air "extra arch":** fDK9WtB3-08 reports an "extra arch to the NE" on Air. All top-down sources disagree, so I trusted the diagrams.
- **Lily-pad arena:** the arena Hl74rVXj2PI calls "air" is filed under Water.
- **Unclear outcomes:** many clips cut before the result, and the outcome column says so.
