## Patterns

**Layout: the Mind arena does NOT fit the KB pillar model.** All four readers agree. The altar is a single round stone about 3 tiles across. A 3-wide two-legged "table" dolmen sits directly S of it, with the guide's Player [0,-2] marked on its north edge (gy70jDTUKXA overview map), and a two-legged archway stands N. Around it, about 3–6 tiles out, is an irregular ring of stonehenge-style uprights, trilithons and loose rocks. There are no clean pillars at (±1,±4)/(±4,±1) and no corner rocks at (±3,±3). Beyond the ring is a large checkerboard-floored outer zone with low graveyard walls, small **walled pens**, headstones and white glowing domes, bounded by a thick sawtooth outer wall. There is no water. Orbs sit 8–17 tiles out, often inside pens or against the outer wall, which look like spawn pockets (NW, W, SW corners). One reader saw orbs stop at the altar edge without scoring, so **the Mind scoring zone may be smaller than 5×5**. This is unconfirmed. A proper Mind map is the biggest gap in this data.

**Recurring angles:**
- **Two-step pull-then-score:** attract the orb out of its pen or corner pocket until it is next to the ring, then either attract again from a home square or swap wands and **repel** it into the altar from outside (8I-SGdZshHQ, sorted by spawn region: "Northern Orbs", "North/South-Eastern Orbs", "Southern Orbs", "South-Western Orbs", "Western Orbs", "North-Western Orbs").
- **Metered attracts:** the eiipycLhN5M captions use "tap", "hold for N", "set" and chained OAO ("4x OAO", "2x OAO, partner scores") to move an orb a fixed number of tiles around walls. They end with "Tap and repel to score" when player, orb and altar are lined up.
- **Pillar deflection** (IZno1q5fIMg): "Angle shots work on any of the pillars": repel past a pillar so the orb slides along it. "Simply line and repel": attract or step until the orb is directly between you and the altar, then repel. Use "tap around" to get orbs out of pens. Tip: click the attract wand in your inventory after each repel.
- **Stacking** (gy70jDTUKXA, A [6,6], B [3,-7], C [-1,-11], Player [0,-2]): A comes straight down the diagonal from S home. B and C are parked together against a stone SE of the altar, then one attract scores both (+2).

**Caveat:** in eiipycLhN5M the compass is barely readable. If its red end isn't north, flip both signs of every tile in that video. Each row there also keeps the raw screen offset.
