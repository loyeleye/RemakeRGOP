# GOP angles: Air altar

Coordinates: the altar centre is (0,0), +x is east, +y is north. Scoring zone is -2..2. Home squares are N (0,2), E (2,0), S (0,-2) and W (-2,0). ## Patterns

**Layout:** this is the best-confirmed altar. The Everheather 19×19 grid diagrams, Icy001's simulator and the "Applying angles" map all match the KB model: arch legs at (±1,±4)/(±4,±1), one-tile gaps at (0,±4)/(±4,0), corner rocks at (±3,±3). The single landmark is a **white portal disc (orb spawn) at the SW, about (-3,-6)**. Use it to find north, because the four arches look alike and most guides don't film north-up. Everheather's videos face W (East guide) or E (West guide), and "Applying angles" plays facing S. Orbs also spawn outside the arches; Everheather numbers them "spots 1–5" running outward along each arch.

**Recurring angles:**
- **Diagonal into a gap:** from any home square, attract an orb whose diagonal leg ends in a gap. For example, (-9,-5)→W home threads (-4,0), (3,-7)→W home threads (0,-4), and orbs on an axis such as (0,7) or (0,-8) go straight through. Shown in XLdDN3KeaOU and PPXA-Xg9qFM, where (7,3)→E home slides past the E arch.
- **From E home (2,0)** (Z0lt3WxvFHM): orbs at (-1,±6) beyond the W leg of the N/S arch, or at (0,±5) in line with a gap: just **hold** from centre. At (-1,±8): step one tile N or S and hold with run off. Directly behind an E leg (5,±1): step one tile N or S and tap once. Portal orbs: step back into the E gap (4,0) and hold with run off, or step S and hold 2 ticks, then centre.
- **From W home (-2,0)** (Wq7IcH74YdU): orbs at (1,±5)/(1,±6) just past the E legs: holding "will not work well", so tap with OAO. At (1,±7): step 1 or 2 tiles away from the orb's side, click, click ground or altar, click again. Corner angle: stand at (-3,±2) beside the corner rock to pull (-1,±5). Behind a W leg (-5,±1): stand diagonally at (-3,∓1) or (-2,∓2) and tap. Rows further out (-6..-9,±1): stand at (-2,∓1), hold until close, then click the altar. Portal orbs: "1 tap".
- **Portal spawns** (lDNcFSXqCX8, from E home): "Make sure walk is on". Portal 1 "step and hold", Portal 2 "step and tap twice", Portal 3 "step, hold for alittle more than a tap, than hold to score it".
- **Whole-round plan** (JQ7P165R5-w): park A [3,-8] and C [4,-4] in a line outside the S gap and B [-8,-3] outside the W arch, then go to N home and pull them straight through (0,-4).
- **Duo/trio play:** E/W duos use fake-repels on orbs behind the E arch to get back to centre sooner (Hl74rVXj2PI). The SW portal spawn can be "deaded" from W/S and set up for E (fDK9WtB3-08).

**Disagreements:** fDK9WtB3-08 reports an "extra arch to the NE" and C-shaped stones on Air, from tilted views. Every top-down source shows the plain four-arch layout, so trust the diagrams.
