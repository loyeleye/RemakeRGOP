# GOP angles: Water altar

Coordinates: the altar centre is (0,0), +x is east, +y is north. Scoring zone is -2..2. Home squares are N (0,2), E (2,0), S (0,-2) and W (-2,0). Rows come from reading video frames, so each row's confidence is about tile placement. Full per-video notes are in `raw/<id>.md` on the capture machine (not in this repo).

Sources: Hl74rVXj2PI (Great Orb Project. Advanced Tricks & Angles - Part 1), fDK9WtB3-08 (Great Orb Project. Advanced Tricks & Angles - Part 2), PPXA-Xg9qFM (Great Orb Project - Some nice to know angles), w_islmRjgP0 (GOP Water Altar Guide), UEXOOPysHQc (Applying angles ~ Water altar #1 ~ GOP)

## Patterns

**Layout:** a grassy island surrounded by water (lily pads), with four trilithon arches and corner rocks at about (±3,±3). It fits the KB model, and UEXOOPysHQc's coordinate card uses the same x=east, y=north convention with Player [0,-2] on S home. Orbs spawn out on the grass spit to the NE, E and SE beside the water, and from a beige portal disc on the W/NW grass about 8–10 tiles out. The lily-pad arena that Hl74rVXj2PI's caption calls "air" is almost certainly Water: fDK9WtB3-08 identifies the lily-pad island as Water, and the forest arena is the real Air. Those rows are filed here and marked "→ probably Water".

**Recurring angles:**
- **Diagonal channels:** in the solo game (w_islmRjgP0) the player stays inside the zone near S home and pulls orbs through the diagonal openings beside the corner rocks more often than under the arches. The video says the angles are general and work on any altar.
- **Puzzle solve** (UEXOOPysHQc, A [6,-10], B [-2,6], C [8,3], Player [0,-2]): B's straight line already enters, so attract it first. Drag A against the S arch and C against the E arch, line them up with the gaps (0,-4) and (4,0), and pull them through. The final push on A is a repel from outside the S arch after a Change-wand. Counter goes 0→3.
- **From E home** (fDK9WtB3-08): a NE orb just outside the NE corner rock (4,4) is attracted, then repelled in ("Note that this trick only works from south."). A SE orb at (4,-4) is attracted while stepping SE and enters in 5 orb-steps, against 6 the "usual" way (drawn tile counts "3, 4, 5!" vs "1, 2 … 6!"). A far N spawn can be "assisted to a northern partner by holding for 2 ticks" or solo-scored "by toggling walk during the drag, premoving south, and double tapping". A far E islet orb gets the repel trick: "set it up for north, … hold for 3 ticks and score it from south yourself, … or repel it in if short on time".
- **Range:** "On water and earth, going west can increase your range."
- **Tawin11** (PPXA-Xg9qFM): orbs at about (-8..-10, 4..5) NW are attracted once from near S home and slide SE in to W home.
