# GOP angles: Body altar

Coordinates: the altar centre is (0,0), +x is east, +y is north. Scoring zone is -2..2. Home squares are N (0,2), E (2,0), S (0,-2) and W (-2,0). Rows come from reading video frames, so each row's confidence is about tile placement. Full per-video notes are in `raw/<id>.md` on the capture machine (not in this repo).

Sources: Hl74rVXj2PI (Great Orb Project. Advanced Tricks & Angles - Part 1), fDK9WtB3-08 (Great Orb Project. Advanced Tricks & Angles - Part 2), PPXA-Xg9qFM (Great Orb Project - Some nice to know angles), JtaLqzvB8bE (GOP Body Altar Guide), I6wZbc3nytk (Applying angles ~ Body altar #1 ~ GOP)

## Patterns

**Layout:** four stone arches at N/E/S/W and small corner rocks at about (±3,±3), matching the KB model (top-down map in I6wZbc3nytk). The arena sits against the N rim of a heart-shaped flesh cave. Cave wall and tendrils hug the N/W/E sides, with open floor to the S. Orbs spawn in the W/NW tendrils and from a **pink portal S/SW of the arena** ("Portal spawns"). Spawns are 6–10 tiles out: E (10,3), SW (-9,-5) and (-5,-8) in the puzzle card.

**Recurring angles:**
- **Chains, not single shots** (JtaLqzvB8bE): "step and tap" from a tile next to the altar to start the orb, then "step again and hold" so the second pull carries it past the corner rock or arch leg. The W side gets most coverage. Several setups bring an orb up to the W arch for a teammate.
- **"Set to middle of the pillars and repel":** stand outside an arch in line with its gap, with the orb in the gap, and repel it straight in.
- **"Step down one" from E home** toggles the angle so an orb in the N gap comes diagonally into the zone.
- **Portal orbs** are staged S of the S arch before scoring.
- **Two orbs on different pillars** (Hl74rVXj2PI): with two orbs just outside an arch, one behind each leg, repel from far outside the arch to push both in. "Fastest way to score two orbs lined up on different pillars".
- **Single-orb solo from behind the S arch** (fDK9WtB3-08): stand at (0,-5) with the orb at (-3,-4) at the W end of the S arch. It takes 3 ticks instead of 4 "however, places you in a poor position". It can also score "while bringing you closer to the 2nd orb". From the W stone: "a useful way to setup this spawn if north is busy."
- **Repel then attract** (PPXA-Xg9qFM): an E orb at about (9,3): "Repel first then switch to attract." The E orb at about (7,-2)→E home is "Same angle as the one shown on fire."
- **Puzzle solve** (I6wZbc3nytk, A [10,3], B [-9,-5], C [-5,-8], Player [0,2]): A is taken with a straight attract that slips N of the E arch leg, then a short finishing pull. B and C are worked round the W side and in through the N arch area. Counter 0→3.

**Caveat:** the Body guide's compass is unreadable, so directions there rest on the cave shape.
