# 09 · Strategy, from beginner to advanced

Each point says where it comes from. Points marked **(engine)** follow directly from the rules in
[05](05-tick-engine.md).

## Beginner: your first game

1. **Stand on a home square** (the middle of one side of the altar) and stay there. "These squares
   are located directly to the north, west, east and south of the center altar" (AnroyRS). They are
   the best spots (RS Wiki Guide).
2. **Pull, don't push.** "You pretty much want to attract them more than repel" (HowToPlayRunescape).
3. **Pull orbs that are along your side of the altar or across it**, not ones directly behind you.
   An orb pulled from behind parks on the tile beside you, outside the zone **(engine)**.
4. **Pull through the gaps in the pillars.** Each side of the altar has a one-tile gap between two
   pillars **(engine/map data)**. "Stand directly behind one of the pillars so that I can attract
   the green orb into this little archway" (AnroyRS).
5. **If an orb gets stuck, let go and pick another.** While you hold it, teammates cannot take it
   (Carousers; engine control rules).
6. **Keep your activity bar up.** Any attract, repel or barrier action refills it. If it empties,
   you are kicked and penalised.
7. **Craft the essence** you get after each altar for double XP. Members should save some for
   Nature.

## Intermediate: playing as a team

* **Split the home squares.** With 2 players, take opposite sides (N + S or E + W). With 3, take any
  three. With 4, take all four. On large altars "there is no agreed upon rule" (RS Wiki Guide). The
  team maps in [maps/](maps/README.md) show how much each setup covers.
* **Relay on large altars.** On Mind and Earth only about 10 to 14% of spawns can be scored from a
  home square without moving. One player walks out and pulls an orb into a teammate's angle, and the
  teammate finishes it: "let me just push it in, or your partner just pulls it in for you"
  (AnroyRS).
* **Moving costs ticks.** A quarter-turn round a small altar costs 3 ticks. Changing sides of a
  large altar can cost 20 or more (RS Wiki Guide). Prefer the orb that is already in your angle.
* **Don't fight teammates for orbs.** The first player to touch an orb owns it while it moves.
  Clicking a teammate's orb wastes your hold **(engine)**.
* **Repel from behind.** Stand so the orb is between you and the zone, and repel. "You can
  essentially stand behind an orb and repel it in a direction that's ideally towards the altar"
  (AnroyRS). Remember the push only works on orbs within 7 tiles **(engine)**.

## Defence

* Until the last 30 seconds you can pull or push the **other team's** orbs. Repelling them away
  from the altar slows them down (RuneHQ). Pushing them into water at Water and Fire (and Chaos)
  can take them out of the round for good (RS Wiki Guide).
* **Barriers** block orbs and players. Put them in **choke points**: the gaps between pillars, not
  open ground (Tip.It, RuneHQ). They do not stop an orb that is already being moved. You get one at
  a time, and enemies need several tries to break yours.
* Many experienced players call defending "pretty much pointless" and "generally frowned upon"
  compared with simply scoring (AnroyRS, 2022). The time is usually better spent on your own orbs.

## Advanced

* **Taps.** A one-tick click moves an orb up to 2 tiles and leaves you free next tick. Chain taps
  across orbs to work two at once (RS Wiki Guide).
* **Prototicks.** Moving and then clicking costs an extra tick; so does the second tick of a new
  hold. Plan clicks so you are already standing still **(engine)**.
* **East/west asymmetry.** Sight lines differ for mirror positions. Learn each side's angles
  separately (RS Wiki Guide; engine).
* **Glitch-repel ("repel tricks").** Swapping wands mid-hold triggers a "pre-attract" step that
  lets you score orbs from positions that look impossible. Wrestling Science documents 6 variations
  in video. The emulator's tests name "Glitchrepel scoring: pillar #3" and "Glitchrepel scoring:
  5-away" (engine).
* **Orb-dragging.** Clicking an orb that is out of reach walks you towards it and fires the attract
  the moment it comes into reach. Used deliberately, it is a fast "walk and pull" in one click
  **(engine)**.
* **Dead orbs.** On Water, Fire and Chaos, a hard repel can strand an orb out of play. On Air, a
  tap-repel can hide one behind the tree (RS Wiki Guide). This works for denial. Be careful with your
  own colour.

## Game formats players use

| Format | What it is | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Random / "100/0" | Ordinary competitive games | RS Wiki, fandom |
| 50/50 | Both teams agree to win half the altars each, which guarantees 405 or 540 tokens | RS Wiki; AnroyRS; HowToPlayRunescape |
| Win orders | 1-1-1-1-1-1-1-1, 4-4, 3-3-1-1 (members); 3-3 on free worlds | Fandom wiki |
| Boosting (2015) | Teams alternate wins Y G Y G... and AFK by holding one orb | AOROX video |
| Crashers | Players who join a 50/50 and play to win anyway | Fandom wiki |

Jagex has said co-operative 50/50 play goes against "the spirit of the game" (fandom wiki quote).
It has not been banned.
