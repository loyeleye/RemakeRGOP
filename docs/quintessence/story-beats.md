# Quintessence: story beats (chapters 1–11)

A summary in our own words of the story of *Quintessence*, an RPG Maker XP game released in chapters
(this build covers 1–11). It was written from a dump of the game's event data made with
`tools/rpgm-dump` (see [HANDOFF.md](HANDOFF.md)). It is a study reference only: nothing here goes into
Lanternfall's story.

**How sure is this?** The chapter order comes from the game's own chapter flags (S76 "Chap2Begins",
S99, S126, S192, S234, S293, S466, S609). Beats inside a chapter come from the biggest cutscenes.
Points marked *(unclear)* are inferences from the dialogue that a playthrough or the full dump should
confirm. Side quests and minor characters are mostly left out.

## World and premise

* **Prion** is a rural kingdom. **Aeria** is a walled, class-divided nation of Quarters run by a High
  Council, with dukes and duchesses who are largely figureheads. The two fought a war that ended years
  before the game. Aerians are recognisable by their hair and eyes, and Quintessence users can sense
  each other nearby.
* **The Quintessence** is a substance taken from the land across the seas. Alden refined it into a pill
  that lets a person **shape-shift** into anyone (or, later, anything) whose hair or feather they hold,
  and **half-shift** to heal their wounds. It is also a slow poison: users die of it after months to
  years. Aeria keeps that secret even from its own users.
* Later chapters reveal that the refined pill is a thin, weak by-product of the real Quintessence,
  which lets beings such as Eshe take elemental forms.

## Chapter 1: Korbin Village

* An opening set about a year later jumps back to ordinary life in Korbin. Reivier hunts, Vikon tags
  along with a bear trap, and a deer chase ends with Vikon swearing off meat (a trap-placing mini-game).
* Jon the merchant spots **Aerian soldiers** heading for the village. The villagers gather at
  Kardian's cabin. Reivier finds Serai with the soldiers at a cliff and pulls a sword on them, but
  they only wanted directions.
* Kardian notices that the story doesn't add up: they were at an isolated cliff, Serai had been gone
  for hours, and she brought back few herbs. Privately he fears Aeria has found him and "Petal".

## Chapter 2: the Duchess in the basement

* That night on the cliff, "Serai" fights Kardian, who subdues her with a poison. Waking in his
  basement lab, she turns out to be **Lunair**, who was wearing Serai's form.
* Kardian reveals that he is **Alden Laeril**, the man who extracted the Quintessence. Flashbacks
  show him summoned to Aeria's Central by Duke Rayne to work on a secret substance, what he saw
  there, and his night escape from Aeria with Petal (a guard-dodging stealth mini-game). In a
  blizzard on the way, Kaire's legs were ruined by frostbite, and Reivier found them.
* Reivier walks in on them and learns that the Serai he has been living with is an Aerian impostor.

## Chapter 3: the bargain

* Kardian tells Lunair the truth: the Quintessence is lethal, Aeria used her as a test subject, and
  she may have months. The only hope is a cure from the land across the seas.
* Lunair names her price. The real Serai is alive in Aeria, and Lunair will return her only if
  someone brings back the cure. Vikon, who overheard everything, volunteers to go with Kardian.
  Salory joins, and they sail from the port. Reivier stays behind to look after Kaire.
* Along the way Reivier helps Lunair recover coins she buried in a valley that has since flooded
  (raft puzzle), and finishes the battle-system tutorial.

## Chapter 4: Reivier takes the pill, and Gaurav Town

* Reivier **steals Kardian's Quintessence and takes it**, so that he can shape-shift too. He asks
  Lunair to take him to Aeria. She refuses until he echoes, without knowing why, a childhood promise
  she once made to a boy named Huey. Shaken, she agrees.
* They need a wagon, so they stop at **Gaurav Town**, which is plagued by a thief who can vanish.
  The Sheriff jails them because Lunair is Aerian. Reivier works with the townsfolk, they find the
  thief's tunnels, and Lunair fights the thief. He is another Aerian who can change into *non-human*
  creatures, which should not be possible with the pill. He gets away, the town gives them a wagon,
  and the drive to Aeria begins. Lunair helps drive.
* A flashback shows Lunair as a lonely child, promising the boy **Huey** that she would play him the
  Prion folk song "To Realize" on her piano.

## Chapter 5: Aeria and the wedding plot

* Kardian's group crosses the sea, with Vikon's bedtime retelling of *The Little Mermaid* on deck.
* Lunair is welcomed home as a hero, and Rayne announces that she will **marry Heath** of the Second
  Quarter in three days, merging the two Quarters. In private, Rayne calls them both tools and has
  Heath give Lunair a mind-binding "love" drug.
* Lunair deals with Heath (he is killed off-screen) and has **Reivier impersonate Heath** using his
  hair. Reivier learns to act like a noble, and a guard-bypass mini-game has the player switch between
  guards' forms to slip past people who know them.
* While Reivier explores the city (side quests in the South and Middle districts), Lunair searches
  for Serai. In a quiet rain scene the two meet in other people's forms, and Lunair starts to soften.

## Chapter 6: Fione and the wedding

* **Fione** finds Lunair at Alden's memorial. She warns her that the Council is using her and hands
  her a remedy for the love drug. Then she seems to lose control of herself, wounds Lunair and
  flees. *(unclear: she appears to be partly controlled by another presence.)*
* At the wedding, just as "Heath" is about to say his vows, news arrives that **Fione is dead**.
  Rayne and Dirk work out that "Heath" may be **Huey**, the boy whose execution Fione had signed off
  on. They seal the city.
* Lunair wants to cut their losses. Reivier refuses to abandon Serai, who is being held in an old
  underground facility as a test subject for Fione's Quintessence samples. They break into the prison,
  fight off waves of guards, and escape on horseback (a riding mini-game). *(unclear: what happened to
  Serai there. Later scenes show Reivier blaming himself, and Lunair gave him a pill to dull the memory.)*

## Chapter 7: across the sea, and the road home

* Kardian, Vikon and Salory arrive in the land across the seas. At night **Eshe** overhears Kardian
  say that he extracted the Quintessence, demands proof, and offers a deal for the cure.
* Lunair and Reivier travel back through the wilderness. Lunair starts **collecting animal hair**
  (wolves, hawk, tiger, bear) so she can turn into animals, which unlocks transformation on the map
  and in battle. Back in Gaurav Town, the tunnels have become an underground village.

## Chapter 8: autumn and winter in Korbin

* Lunair, wearing Serai's form for the villagers, lives quietly in Korbin with Reivier while they wait
  for the cure. She learns to fly, they watch clouds, and both face how little time they and Kaire
  have left.
* The village visits a **winter carnival** (several mini-games and a fortune teller who tells Lunair
  she has no future).
* Kaire is dying, and before she goes she confesses, in a run of wagon flashbacks: she is **not Petal**.
  As children, Petal and another girl were riding together to meet Petal's brother Alden, whom
  Petal had never seen. Kaire was that other girl. Petal died, and the survivor took her name and lived as Alden's sister so that he
  would not be alone. Lunair persuades Reivier to keep this from Kardian.
* Vikon and Kardian return with **two doses** of the cure. Reivier takes one and loses his powers. The
  other goes wrong: by Chapter 9, Lunair is stuck in the form of a **black hawk**. *(unclear: the exact
  scene.)*

## Chapter 9: Serai returns

* The real **Serai** is home, and Salory reunites with her. They keep Lunair's existence a secret,
  so the hawk is passed off as an injured bird that Reivier and Serai nursed. Vikon wins Salory over to
  keeping "her" with a tall tale.
* Vikon notices that Reivier has changed. Puzzle-heavy flashbacks follow: Serai and Lunair, platform
  and light puzzles, and a golem battle with Eshe.

## Chapter 10: Trindle Village, Shanti and the ambush

* Lunair wakes after **two months** unconscious. Kardian explains that a being called **Shanti**
  has lived inside her since birth. Fione picked it up on the expedition that first brought back the
  Quintessence, and Lunair's dose of raw Quintessence woke it. When Shanti stirs, the people hunting it
  can sense where Lunair is, so the party has to keep moving.
* Reivier has **taken the Quintessence again** so that he can keep going on the journey. *(unclear: why
  exactly; he also helped get Lunair "the eye".)* Lunair pushes him to stay home, and he insists that
  this is now his responsibility too. Serai insists on coming.
* A childhood flashback shows young Reivier and Serai finding "that place", their cliff. Eshe and
  Ailah show the villagers illusions (the rat and snake pranks) to explain what the real Quintessence
  can do.
* In the Southeast Woods, **Rayne, Dirk and Agatha** ambush the party. Eshe and Ailah take on Agatha,
  and the others fight the soldiers. Vikon is badly hurt *(unclear: exact fate)*.

## Chapter 11: toward the source

* In Gaurav Town, Kardian says goodbye to Lunair as a father would. He gives her the key to his lab
  under the Korbin cabin and leaves to do something he "is not proud of".
* The party (Reivier, Lunair, Serai, Eshe, Ailah) travels by wagon toward Prion's coast and then
  across the sea. Eshe explains that the real Quintessence comes from a time and place even she has
  never seen.
* **Dream sequences** show Agatha as a child raised to see sacrifice as an honour, and a council of her
  people debating a plan to raise a bloodline's Quintessence dose generation after generation until a
  descendant becomes a **new source**.
* Underwater gathering in bubble suits, and quiet night talks: Lunair asks whether the harm to Vikon
  is her fault, and Reivier imagines a peaceful life in Korbin with her in it. The build ends partway
  through this chapter.

## Themes worth noting (for study)

* **Identity and names:** shape-shifting, impersonation, Kaire living as Petal ("what's in a name"),
  and Reivier unknowingly being Huey.
* **Bargains and promises:** Lunair's hostage deal, the childhood piano promise that echoes years
  later, and the vows at a fake wedding.
* **A tonal mix:** heavy reveals are cut with Vikon's comic storytelling, and the cast is a found
  family in a small village.
