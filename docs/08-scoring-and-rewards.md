# 08 · Scoring, objectives and rewards

## The objective, in one line

Win more altars than the other team. You win an altar by having **more orbs of your colour touch
it** in its 2 minutes than the other team gets of theirs.

## Inside one altar

| Event | Effect |
|---|---|
| Your colour's orb enters the 5 × 5 zone round the altar | +1 to your team when it registers 2 ticks later ([05 §2.4](05-tick-engine.md#24-how-an-orb-steps-orbstep)); a new orb spawns |
| The other colour's orb enters the zone | +1 to **them**, whoever pushed it |
| Time runs out (200 ticks) | Higher score wins the altar; equal scores draw |

There is no penalty for pushing an orb into the wrong altar zone, because orbs only ever count for
their own colour. Defence means keeping the other colour's orbs **away**.

## Tokens (Runecrafting Guild tokens)

Paid at the end of the game. "All rounds of the minigame (8 for members and 6 for free-to-play) need
to be completed to receive the reward." (RS Wiki, *Runecrafting guild token*)

| Per altar, per player | Tokens |
|---|---|
| Win | 100 |
| Draw | 25 |
| Loss | 10 |

| End-of-game bonus to the team that won more altars | Members | Free |
|---|---|---|
| Majority bonus | 200 | 150 |
| Tied on altars (split evenly) | 100 each | 75 each |

* **Maximum per game:** 1,000 on members worlds (8 × 100 + 200) and 750 on free worlds
  (6 × 100 + 150).
* **"50/50" game** (each team wins exactly half): free 3 × 100 + 3 × 10 + 75 = **405**; members
  4 × 100 + 4 × 10 + 100 = **540** (derived). These match the RS Wiki's "405 tokens per F2P game" and
  "540 tokens per P2P game", and the 2015 boosting guide's "540 tokens". A 2008 video's "610 tokens"
  for a members 50/50 does not fit the current rates. It may predate a change.
* **You must take part.** You get nothing for an altar unless you attracted or repelled at least
  one orb (either colour) there. Barriers alone do not count, though they keep your activity bar up
  (RS Wiki).
* **Early end:** if the game collapses because too many people left, the remaining players are paid
  as if they had **lost** the remaining altars.
* RuneHQ (2008-2012) also mentions a variable "quality of play" bonus. No other source does.
* Tokens are untradeable and stackable. Item ID 13650. Examine: "Wizard Elriss will exchange these
  for rewards."
* Thalers (RuneScape's generic minigame currency) are also earned. AnroyRS (2022) says 1 Thaler per
  5 minutes, and the 2015 boosting guide says 20 Thalers per members game.

## Essence and experience during the game

* After each altar, everyone gets essence: **team score − half the other team's score + 1, minimum
  1** (RS Wiki; rounding not stated). Rune essence on free worlds and pure essence on members
  worlds. Extra drops on the ground if your pack is full.
* Runes crafted at GOP altars give **double** base XP. Runecrafting gloves or bonus XP double it
  again (the RS Wiki phrasing is "3× for a winner with either"). The old fandom text says Air gloves
  turn 5 XP into 15.
* Explorer's rings 2-4 give extra runes at the elemental altars. Members are advised to save essence
  for Nature (and Chaos), where it is worth most.

### Outfits

| Item | Tokens | Effect |
|---|---|---|

Master runecrafter set bonus: the shop text says "Grants 1% bonus Runecrafting XP while worn. A full
set grants an additional 1% bonus" (so 5% in total). The main article and the 2015 video agree on
**+5% for the full set**. RuneHQ lists 0.4/0.8/0.6/0.6% per piece. A full set costs 60,000 tokens:
about 148 free 50/50 games or 111 members 50/50 games (RS Wiki).

### Omni-talisman (members, not a token item)

Show Elriss one of each of the 13 regular talismans or tiaras, once, for 13,605 Runecrafting XP and
the omni-talisman, which opens all 13 talisman altars. Tip.It gives the per-talisman XP: Air 9,
Mind 9, Water 11, Earth 17, Fire 27, Body 50, Cosmic 109, Chaos 216, Nature 531, Law 1,428,
Death 4,242, Blood 6,957. That is 12 talismans totalling 13,606, one more than the Wiki's 13,605 for 13; Tip.It predates the Soul altar. Wizard Korvak turns it into an
omni-tiara or omni-talisman staff. A lost one costs 50,000 coins to replace (since 27 May 2025).

### Ironmen

Since 5 January 2015, Ironman accounts may play, but only get the cosmetic robes and a single Blood
altar teleport. No master robes or staff.

## Achievement

**Altar Explorer** (10 RuneScore): play a round on each different altar map.
