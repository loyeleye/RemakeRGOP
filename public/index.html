Lanternfall

Up to four lamplighter guilds. One shrine brazier. Pull your wisps in, push theirs away.

Story progress (testing)

How to play
  • The shrine runs on a clock: every 0.6 s is one tick. A round is 200 ticks.
  • Tap a wisp to reach for it with your staff (10 tiles, in sight). Keep holding the action and it keeps drifting 2 tiles at a time. On a laptop, press O for big taps: every wisp gets a larger ringed target, so it is hard to miss. O again turns it off.
  • Pull works on your guild's wisps. Push (Q) works on any wisp, from 8 tiles.
  • A wisp that sits on the glowing rim for 2 ticks is banked for its guild and respawns.
  • Tap the ground to walk. Run (R) moves 2 tiles a tick. If you step into reach, you steady for one tick before your staff takes hold.
  • A wisp in flight belongs to whoever flung it. Rivals can only push once it settles.
  • Camera: scroll, pinch or press + and − to zoom, drag or use the arrow keys to look around, and press C or ◎ to come back to yourself.
